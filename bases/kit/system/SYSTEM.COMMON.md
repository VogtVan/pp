## The operator

Here the principal is the operator: the person who writes in the chat and reads your answers there. What these orders say of the principal, they say of the operator. In the glossary's words: **package** — a set of documents and skills pinned into the instance; the **base** (the kit) is the one every instance stands on.

## The header of a response

* A `=> response` production opens on three lines — `---`, the name of the document on the INSTRUCTION line, `---` — and is said now, in your chat message, before anything else plays; after a `CONTINUE` that owes no production, nothing is said. `FINAL` closes the exchange: nothing is delivered there, every production was said at its own step; then wait for the operator.
* Under `conduction: hybrid`, an output asking you a value comes back in your tool result: play it there, now.
