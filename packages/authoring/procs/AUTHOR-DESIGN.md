# AUTHOR-DESIGN — the instance answers the ask

Work through the hall's cycle; what is yours here:

1. **Read the ask against the doctrine** served in the hall: answer the author's seven questions
   in a few lines before anything is sketched.
2. **Choose the patterns** from the catalogue -- an overlay of TURN for a reflex and an offer, a
   proc behind a door or a mount for a method, a document at a socket for induced or background
   work, a payload or a mount for data, a workflow for a path, a loop for laps -- each by its line
   in the catalogue, and name each one you take.
3. **Count the cost** of the document every turn and every run; for the same result, the lighter
   wins.
4. **Sketch** the document whole -- front matter, proc, body -- and close the sketch on one table:
   what it declares, what pp guarantees for it, what it costs.
5. **After the writing**, `./pp -build` and `./pp <key> -peek` render what a real call would show.
