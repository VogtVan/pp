# `PP-DESIGN` — the instance evolves to answer the ask

The catalog and the theory were served above. You are the author now: take the operator's ask and make this instance answer it with pp's own mechanisms — an overlay, a proc of the instance, a door, a contributor, a workflow — never with a habit the next session forgets. Work in order:

1. **Read the ask against the theory.** Say where the work comes from — direct, induced or background —, at what moment the agent needs the knowledge (before the ask or after it), what keeps it silent when nothing is due, and what is scriptable: a skill's, never the agent's. The checklist that closes `CONDUCTION` is the seven questions; answer them in a few lines before anything is written.
2. **Choose the mechanism at the catalog.** Name the figure that answers — an overlay of TURN for a reflex and an offer, a proc under a door or a mounted document for a method, a document at a socket for induced or background work, a payload or a mount for data, a workflow for a path, a described door for laps —, then describe the document by its forms before writing it (PD1). Count what it costs every turn and every run; equal result, the lighter wins (PD2). Conducted or neutral: the intended initiative decides (PD3).
3. **Propose the complete document.** Its front matter written by `pp-authoring` — `section set` for a key, `section constrain` for the laws (the codes are the script's, never yours), `section serve` for the readings —, its body indented four spaces as the catalog shows its examples (PD4); it says the present and speaks its own layer (PD7, PD8); its laws short, load-bearing first (PD5). Place each piece deliberately: a step's position, an offer's position in a fused output, a law's position in its list each change what plays. A public name wears its package's name (`pp-<package>` for a skill, `<package>_<key>` for a key); a proc or a ref stays bare. Close on one table: what the document declares, what the engine guarantees, what it costs.
4. **Install on the operator's GO alone, and prove it played.** Nothing lands before the word (PD6). Once placed, `./pp -build` refuses the lies by name and `./pp <key> -peek` renders the block without effect; when the bench cannot play the document, a throwaway instance plays it end to end. What that rendering teaches is said, and the document amended before the GO is asked again.

## What this door is not

Not the catalog — the served `PP_CHEATSHEET` is, and the package's bench plays it. Not the theory — the served `CONDUCTION` is. Not the agent's contract — the card in the standing rules is. Not a validator — the engine is.
