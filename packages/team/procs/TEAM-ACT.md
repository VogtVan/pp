# TEAM-ACT — the form of a team act

A team reads the messages of its members in the `words` they say; a `words` that is one `team-act` value is an act, routed as soon as it is read. The format is declared here, once for the house and every agent it holds; the contract of the acts -- the three forms, the debts, the discipline of emission -- is served to the members by the team's document, `TEAM-<name>.md`, at the start of each of their exchanges.
