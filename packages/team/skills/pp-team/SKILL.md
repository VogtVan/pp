# `pp-team` — the house's hand over its teams

## The hand, in one breath

The house's hand over its teams, and its guards -- `start` reads a team's declaration (teams/<name>/TEAM.md) whole, gives birth to the agents its members need -- `<name> <path>` per member, several members on one agent, each path born once under the house's birth lock -- and writes in the team's directory (`teams/<name>/`, the root of documents of every run the team opens -- nothing written in an agent's instance) the head of the team's document `TEAM-<name>.md` -- its body the team's intention, the owner's, served to every run of the team; the contract of the acts is this package's fragment of the standing orders -- and `TURN.md`, the overlay of the kit's turn that makes every exchange of the team's runs ask for the form `team-act`, opens its first task and lays the house's ONE relaunch entry when absent -- the way back of its teams after a restart; the NET under every other trigger is the team's timer, at the step of `schedule:`, `off` for none --; `post` adds a task, an info, a reply or a stop at any time, and lays the entry again when it finds it gone; `post --every` lays a RECURRENCE on a task to come -- the tick opens one task per period, skipped while the one before is still open; the period is a day and an hour, or a duration (`4h`) whose first task opens at the pose; `recurrence` changes its hour or its day -- each date counted once, the new hour from its next period --, its context limit or its text -- the occurrences to come alone --, or lays a recurrence on a task that exists, the task its last occurrence, and `stop --recurrence` ends it; the run of a recurrence is replaced by a new one at the first wake-up of a new occurrence once the context that run last measured reached the limit `--context` set; the run of a task is replaced at the wake-up after it parks once its last measure reached the task's limit (`post --context`) or else the season's (`start --context`, `context:` of SEASON.md); a member's run is replaced when the season gives the member another model or provider than the one the run was opened under; `stop` ends the season -- the open tasks stopped, the recurrences silenced, the team OFF SEASON until the next `start` --, or with `--task` posts the stop to every agent holding the task -- delivered at once, nobody woken for it --, returns what was left undelivered and, under `--kill`, ends the wake-ups under way; `reset` forgets every run of a member, so that its next wake-up on a task opens a new one told that task's history; `ring` rings a ceremony at hand, out of its hour, under the period `manual-<id>`, and a ceremony line's `any <n>` is its quorum -- said to the facilitator in its consigne, holding nothing; `pause` holds the team -- nothing killed, the wake-ups under way end on their own, and until `resume <name>` takes it back the tick wakes nobody and rings no ceremony --; `resume --task` takes back a task a guard left with nobody due -- stopped, held by a final failure or past `launches:` --, its counters counted afresh and its members informed, the team's budget never reset; `resume --quota` lifts a provider's limit at once, dated or not -- one member's with `--member`, else every member's a limit holds -- and the tick launches each member lifted again; `budget` raises the team's budget or `budget-launches` without a start, the members keeping their runs, and each wake-up gets its share of the budget's rest, reserved while it plays; `declaration` reads a team's `TEAM.md` with its verdict and its gap to the start, writing nothing; `templates` lists the templates the house sees -- its own `.pp/teams/templates/` then the package's -- and `templates --render` shows one document as `start` would write it, writing nothing; `document` prints the source of one document the house uses and is the one writer of the house's own documents -- `--new` creates one from a skeleton, `--body-file` replaces its text once checked, `--delete` removes it while no line of `documents:` names it; the package's documents can be read, never changed --; and `declare` writes a team's values -- `TEAM.md`, the team born or standing, or `SEASON.md` under `--season` --, the keys its flags name and they alone, judged before any write, never in season; `delete` removes a team and what it made -- its timer, its relaunch entry, the runs its wake-ups opened, its journal, its directory --, the agents left standing; `guard` keeps `agent purge` off the run a member of a living team holds -- this package guards the family `agent-run`; `tick` advances the team (reap, route, ring, wake) without a model -- a wake-up is ONE launch (`agent start --launches 1`), the head it writes before pp's output the one delivery, each act a `words` of the launch carries -- alone or among other text -- routed as it comes; it RINGS each ceremony once per period (one task from the team to its facilitator, the participants delegated) and the document a wake-up's trigger names (`entries:`) is a SUCCESSOR of the agent's procedure, elected at every exchange by the team's document in its directory, whose decider is `entry` --, within the limits the declaration sets (`parallel`, `budget`, `retries`); it breaks a DEADLOCK -- a member at the bottom of the deadlocked set that owes an answer on a task that does not halt it is relanced while it owes, without a count, and its SILENCE makes it suspected: `silence:` PASSAGES in a row -- a passage is a wake-up and its continuations, until the run parks -- that end without one act of its while it owed an answer on the task at the wake-up; a passage that ends while it waits for a principal's answer counts for nothing (team 1.33.0); the head of each wake-up says how many exchanges are left, this one counted; a standing run is continued while it does not park and counts for nothing: `launches:` and `budget:` are its only bounds --; it SUSPECTS a member whose wake-up failed for good -- for an interval that doubles at each final failure in a row, every debt it owes and every `do` or `ask` reaching it answered in its place -- the message closed and handed back to its author, whose next head shows it whole, with its vector --, a principal's opening told to that principal once by the team's notice, nobody waking it; past the interval its next message tries it, and a try that answers ends the suspicion; the head of every other wake-up says who is suspected --; the tick and `stop` remove the house's relaunch entry when no team of the house needs its net any more; the guards hold at the tick -- a wake-up stuck past `timeout:` seconds is killed and FAILS under the retries, `launches:` bounds one task's launches and `budget-launches:` the season's (each said once, the owner escalated to, the task left open), and `snapshot` writes the replayed state in ONE event so the state readers restart there (the resolved tasks' messages pruned at the pack; nothing rewritten, every reader byte-identical); `status`, `watch`, `log`, `result` and `responses` read without advancing -- `status --json` is the face a program reads, one JSON object per team, and `log`, `responses` and `inbox` take `--json` too; `inbox` says what waits for the owner and feeds the TEAM_INBOX contributor at the owner's turn; the head of a wake-up does not list the others' answers: `responses` reads them. The one writer of each team's journal; the wake-ups it launches are the house's console's; the scheduler is written on the owner's commands and in the tick alone, never by the engine.
