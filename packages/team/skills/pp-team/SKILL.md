# `pp-team` — the house's hand over its teams

## The hand, in one breath

The house's hand over its teams, and its guards -- `start` reads a team's declaration (teams/<name>/TEAM.md) whole, gives birth to the agents its members need -- `<name> <path>` per member, several members on one agent, each path born once under the house's birth lock -- and writes in the team's directory (`teams/<name>/`, the root of documents of every run the team opens -- nothing written in an agent's instance) the team's law on its answer (an overlay of the kit's TURN -- the TURN is ONE JSON object), opens its first task and lays the team's ONE line in the operating system's calendar (`schedule:` minutes, `off` for none; the NET under every other trigger); `post` adds a task, a note, a reply or a stop at any time, and lays the line again when it finds it gone with a task open; `stop` posts the stop to every agent holding the task -- delivered at once, nobody woken for it --, returns what was left undelivered and, under `--kill`, ends the wake-ups under way; `reset` forgets a member's run, so that its next wake-up opens a new one told every open task; `ring` rings a ceremony at hand, out of its hour, under the period `manual-<id>`, and a ceremony line's `any <n>` is its quorum -- the facilitator woken at the n-th answer; `pause` holds the team -- nothing killed, the wake-ups under way end on their own, and until `resume <name>` takes it back the tick wakes nobody and rings no ceremony --; `resume --task` takes back a task a guard left with nobody due -- stopped, held by a final failure or past `wakes:` --, its counters counted afresh and its members noted, the team's budget never reset; `budget` raises the team's budget or `budget-wakes` without a start, the members keeping their runs, and each wake-up gets its share of the budget's rest, reserved while it plays; `declaration` reads a team's `TEAM.md` with its verdict and its gap to the start, writing nothing; `templates` lists the templates a team starts from and `new` writes a team's `TEAM.md` from one, judged by `start`'s own judge before any write; `guard` keeps `agent purge` off the run a member of a living team holds -- this package guards the family `agent-run`; `tick` advances the team (reap, route, waits, ring, wake) without a model -- it RINGS each ceremony once per period (one task from the team to its facilitator, the participants delegated) and the document a wake-up's trigger names (`entries:`) is a SUCCESSOR of the agent's procedure, elected at every exchange by the team's document in its directory, whose decider is `entry` --, within the limits the declaration sets (`parallel`, `budget`, `retries`) and the `await` an answer declares; it breaks a DEADLOCK -- a member at the bottom of the deadlocked set that owes an answer is relanced, and a debt two relances carried is answered in its place, never a principal's opening --; it SUSPECTS a member whose wake-up failed for good -- for an interval that doubles at each final failure in a row, every debt it owes and every `do` or `ask` reaching it answered in its place, a principal's opening told to that principal once by the team's notice, nobody waking it; past the interval its next message tries it, and a try that answers ends the suspicion; the head of every other wake-up says who is suspected --; the tick and `stop` remove the calendar line when no task stays open and no ceremony is declared; the guards hold at the tick -- a wake-up stuck past `timeout:` seconds is killed and FAILS under the retries, `wakes:` bounds one task's wake-ups and `budget-wakes:` the team's total (each said once, the owner escalated to, the task left open), and `snapshot` writes the replayed state in ONE event so the state readers restart there (the resolved tasks' messages pruned at the pack; nothing rewritten, every reader byte-identical); `status`, `watch`, `log`, `result` and `responses` read without advancing -- `status --json` is the face a program reads, one JSON object per team, and `log`, `responses` and `inbox` take `--json` too; `inbox` says what waits for the owner and feeds the TEAM_INBOX contributor at the owner's turn; the head of a wake-up names what the others answered since the agent's last wake-up. The one writer of each team's journal; the wake-ups it launches are the house's console's; the calendar is written on the owner's commands and in the tick alone, never by the engine.

## Commands

The house's console carries the verb: `./pp team <verb> …` at the house's root. An
agent of the house reaches the verbs through the conductor: `./pp <key> -s pp-team <verb> …`.

| `guard` | The GUARDIAN of the family `agent-run` (`agent-run: {guard: pp-team}`), the engine's call when `agent purge` asks before removing a run: the vector `agent-run:<path>/<key>` on stdin -- a run a member of a LIVING team holds (the team started, a task open or a ceremony declared) refuses `member-run`, the team, the member and `team reset <team> --member <member>` named; rc 0 otherwise; `family-foreign` for another family. Reads only, no lock. |
| `budget <name> [<usd>] [--wakes <n>] [--as <principal>] [--no-tick]` | RAISES the team's `budget` (dollars) or `budget-wakes` (wake-ups) without a start. Judged under the journal's lock before anything is written: `team-unknown`, `team-not-started`, `principal-unknown`, `budget-invalid` (a number at or below what the team spent since its last start -- the cost kept, the wake-ups launched -- or not a number); the usage without a number nor `--wakes`. Then `budget-raised {budget, wakes, by}`: the replay takes the new bounds and lifts `budget-spent` -- the next exhaustion writes it again --; the members keep their runs. Says `raised #<id> · budget <usd> USD · budget-wakes <n>`; then the calendar line laid again while a task is open; then a tick, unless `--no-tick`: the members due woken in their own runs. |
| `ring <name> <ceremony> [--as <principal>] [--no-tick]` | Rings ONE declared ceremony AT HAND, out of its hour. Judged under the journal's lock before anything is written: `team-unknown`, `team-not-started`, `principal-unknown` (`--as` names neither a mandator nor the owner), `ceremony-unknown` (a name `ceremonies:` does not declare; the declared ones said), `ceremony-open` (a task of the ceremony, rung by the tick or at hand, still open: one ring at a time); the usage without a ceremony. Then the tick's own ring (step 4 of `tick`) under the period `manual-<id>` -- the id of its own `ceremony-due`, which carries `by` (the principal) --: ONE task from the team to the facilitator, its opening `do` to the facilitator, a `do` per participant, and the quorum's `wait` under `any <n>`. The period is no date: the `every` ceremony rings its day at its hour all the same; an `after` ceremony follows a ring at hand as any other, its period inherited. Says `<ceremony> rung at hand · manual-<id> #<id> · task <T> to <facilitator>`; then the calendar line laid again (unless `schedule: off`); then a tick, unless `--no-tick`, which wakes the participants. |
| `declaration <name> [--json]` | READS the declaration: `TEAM.md` whole, the verdict of `start`'s own judge -- `ok`, or the refusal `start` would write, said with rc 0 (a broken `TEAM.md` is shown to be mended, never refused) -- and its GAP to the last start: `{key, started, declared}` per key of the front matter `team-started` kept that the file now says otherwise (`agents`, `reports`, `root`, `principals`, `ceremonies` by their lines, `entries`, `work`, `limits`, `schedule`) -- what a new `start` would change; the body is never pending, pp serves it at once. Under `--json` ONE object `{team, path, text, digest, verdict: {ok, refusal}, started: {at, id, digest} | null, changed, pending, declared}` (`pending` null under a refused verdict; `declared` -- the front matter as `start`'s judge reads it, what the next start writes: `{agents [{member, path, born, packages}], root, reports, principals, limits, schedule, ceremonies, entries, work}`, the members in the order declared -- each with `born` (an instance stands at its path) and `packages` `[{name, on}]`, the packages it plays: for an agent born, what its instance pins beyond its base as its SETTINGS switch them; for one not born yet, what its birth gives, the house's pins beyond its base, each on (team 0.38.0) --, the limits of `TEAM.md` alone (never a season's), the ceremonies' lines; null under a refused verdict (team 0.32.2)); the text says the path, the text, `verdict ok` or the refusal, then a line per key pending, or `not started`. Nothing written, no lock, never a tick; `team-unknown`, the usage. |
| `templates [--json]` | The TEMPLATES a team starts from -- `supervisor-workers`, `lead-reviewers`, `chain`, `solo`, `empty`, `council`, `triage`, `cross-check`, `tree` --, one line each: its name, its default members, what it is; under `--json` one object per line, `{template, description, members, root, reports, ceremony, limits, intention}`. Nothing written. |
| `new <name> --template <t> [--member <name> <path>]... [--root <member>] [--principal <p>]... [--budget <usd>] [--wakes <n>] [--timeout <s>] [--schedule <minutes>\|off] [--no-ceremony] [--intention-file <path>] [--squads <n>]` | WRITES `teams/<name>/TEAM.md` from a template (`templates/<t>.md` of the package): `--member` replaces the default members (`agents/<name>/<member>`) in the order given and the template's FORM makes the root, the edges and the names of the intention of them -- the first member or `--root` the root, the others reporting to it (`supervisor-workers`, `lead-reviewers`: the daily when two or more do, unless `--no-ceremony`; `council`, `triage`, `cross-check`: no daily) or each to the one before it (`chain`); `tree` takes the `--squads <n>` members after the root as its squad leads (two unless said), reporting to the root, and gives the members after them to the squad leads in turn as their workers, the daily of the squad leads when they are two or more -- `team-invalid` under one worker per squad lead, and for `--squads` off `tree` or below 1; `solo` and `empty` one member; the limits `budget: 5`, `wakes: 8`, `timeout: 900` (`tree`: `budget: 10`, `wakes: 16`), `schedule: off` unless given. Starts nothing; never writes over a team. |
| `entry <name> [--run <key>]` | The document the team's wake-up under that run -- `PP_RUN` when `--run` is absent -- plays this exchange, on stdout, one name: the last wake-up of the team launched under the key and not ended -- found by its intent's `run`, its `wake-launched`, else its tag at the house's `messages.jsonl` (an opening's first exchange plays before the tick writes `wake-launched`) --; its task's `ceremony` names `entries[<ceremony>]`, any other task `entries[do]`; no entry, no such wake-up, no journal: `TEAM-IDLE.md`, so that the agent's exchange always goes on. The decider of the team's document: the engine of the agent plays it at every exchange, under `PP_RUN` and `PP_HOME` -- the house is the machine this script lives in, never the instance served. Nothing written, no lock; without `--run` nor `PP_RUN`: the usage. |

Every event is appended under `record.held` with the next id, read under the same lock;
nothing is ever rewritten. A reader skips a last line still being written; a line that does
not parse anywhere else refuses by its name (`line n of journal.jsonl is not a record`). The
tick keeps no clock and launches nothing but by the house's console; the calendar line is the
clock's: cron replays it every `schedule:` minutes, and this script lays and removes it.

## Refusals

Refusals exit 2 and write nothing.

| Cause | What is said |
|---|---|
| a team's name that is no slug, or no `TEAM.md` under `teams/<name>/` | `team-invalid` · `team-unknown` |
| a declaration the engine or this contract cannot read, or a limit of the wrong form (`parallel` under 1, `budget` not above 0, `retries` under 0, a `schedule` neither `off` nor a whole number of 1 or more, a `timeout` not above 0, a `wakes` or `budget-wakes` under 1, a word) | `team-invalid`, the line or the key and its value said |
| a ceremony whose when is not `every day\|weekday\|mon..sun HH:MM` or `after <ceremony>`, or without `with a b -> c`, or whose `any <n>` is not a whole number from 1 to its participants; an entry naming neither `do` nor a declared ceremony | `team-invalid`, the line said |
| `ring` on a team never started, a ceremony nobody declared, a ceremony whose task is still open; without a ceremony | `team-not-started` · `ceremony-unknown` · `ceremony-open` · the usage |
| a `PP_NOW` that is no ISO date-time | `now-invalid` |
| no `root`, or one nobody declared | `root-missing` · `participant-unknown` |
| an edge, a ceremony or `--to` naming an agent nobody declared | `participant-unknown` |
| `reset` while a tick holds `tick.lock` past `call_lock_wait`; while a wake-up of a member named plays or its run's wake lock is held; an agent's console that keeps the run | `team-busy` · `member-running` · `reset-failed` (the members forgotten before it written) |
| ceremonies whose `after` loop | `ceremony-cycle` |
| `start` with a `TEAM.md` dropping a member that holds an open task | `member-held` |
| `start` with `--budget`/`--budget-wakes` not a positive number; `--package` not `<name>=on\|off`, switching off `team` or what it requires, naming a package not pinned | `budget-invalid` · `package-invalid` · `package-required` · `package-unpinned` |
| `start` with `--harness` and no `--provider` beside it | `grammar-missing` |
| `watch` with a heartbeat under 1 or a bound under 0, `log` with `--last` under 1 | `heartbeat-invalid` · `for-invalid` · `last-invalid` |
| `result` on a task open or stopped, on a team without a resolved task | `task-open` · `result-missing` |
| `inbox --seen` without a name, or on a team never started | the usage · `team-not-started` |
| `responses` on a team never started, with `--since` or `--last` under 0 | `team-not-started` · `since-invalid` · `last-invalid` |
| an agent that could not be born under the house | `agent-add-failed`, the engine's refusal |
| a path another call is giving birth to past `call_lock_wait` | `born-busy` |
| `post` or `stop` on a team never started, on a task no event opened, on a closed task | `team-not-started` · `task-unknown` · `task-closed` |
| `resume --task` on a team never started, on a task no event opened, on a resolved task, past the team's budget, on an open task nothing blocks | `team-not-started` · `task-unknown` · `task-resolved` · `budget-spent` · `task-not-blocked` |
| `pause` on a team never started, on a team paused already; `resume` without `--task` on a team not paused | `team-not-started` · `team-paused` · `team-not-paused` |
| `budget` on a team never started; a number at or below what the team spent since its start (dollars, wake-ups launched), or not a number; without a number nor `--wakes` | `team-not-started` · `budget-invalid` · the usage |
| `--as` naming neither a mandator nor the owner | `principal-unknown` |
| `--reply` citing no post of the task | `message-unknown` |
| a post without its message, `--reply` without `--task`, `--reply` with `--stop` | `message-missing` · the usage |
| a journal line that does not parse, elsewhere than last | `line n of journal.jsonl is not a record` |
| an entry neither in the team's directory nor resolved by the instance of an agent whose members play it, at `start` | `entry-missing`, each agent and document named |
| any other call | the usage, one line |

A wake-up's own failures are never refusals: they are `wake-failed` events of the journal; a kill is a `wake-killed` event, and a pid gone before it is said, not refused.
