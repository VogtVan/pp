# `pp-team` — the house's hand over its teams

## The hand, in one breath

The house's hand over its teams, and its guards -- `start` reads a team's declaration (teams/<name>/TEAM.md) whole, gives birth to the agents its members need -- `<name> <path>` per member, several members on one agent, each path born once under the house's birth lock -- and writes in the team's directory (`teams/<name>/`, the root of documents of every run the team opens -- nothing written in an agent's instance) the head of the team's document `TEAM-<name>.md` -- its body the team's intention, the owner's, served to every run of the team; the contract of the acts is this package's fragment of the standing orders --, opens its first task and lays the team's ONE line in the operating system's calendar (`schedule:` minutes, `off` for none; the NET under every other trigger); `post` adds a task, a note, a reply or a stop at any time, and lays the line again when it finds it gone with a task open; `stop` posts the stop to every agent holding the task -- delivered at once, nobody woken for it --, returns what was left undelivered and, under `--kill`, ends the wake-ups under way; `reset` forgets a member's run, so that its next wake-up opens a new one told every open task; `ring` rings a ceremony at hand, out of its hour, under the period `manual-<id>`, and a ceremony line's `any <n>` is its quorum -- the facilitator woken at the n-th answer; `pause` holds the team -- nothing killed, the wake-ups under way end on their own, and until `resume <name>` takes it back the tick wakes nobody and rings no ceremony --; `resume --task` takes back a task a guard left with nobody due -- stopped, held by a final failure or past `launches:` --, its counters counted afresh and its members noted, the team's budget never reset; `budget` raises the team's budget or `budget-launches` without a start, the members keeping their runs, and each wake-up gets its share of the budget's rest, reserved while it plays; `declaration` reads a team's `TEAM.md` with its verdict and its gap to the start, writing nothing; `templates` lists the templates a team starts from and `new` writes a team's `TEAM.md` and `TEAM-<name>.md` from one, judged by `start`'s own judge before any write; `delete` removes a team and what it made -- its timer, its calendar line, the runs its wake-ups opened, its journal, its directory --, the agents left standing; `guard` keeps `agent purge` off the run a member of a living team holds -- this package guards the family `agent-run`; `tick` advances the team (reap, route, waits, ring, wake) without a model -- a wake-up is ONE launch (`agent start --launches 1`), the head it writes before pp's output the one delivery, each `words` of the launch that is a `team-act` value routed as it comes; it RINGS each ceremony once per period (one task from the team to its facilitator, the participants delegated) and the document a wake-up's trigger names (`entries:`) is a SUCCESSOR of the agent's procedure, elected at every exchange by the team's document in its directory, whose decider is `entry` --, within the limits the declaration sets (`parallel`, `budget`, `retries`) and the `await` an answer declares; it breaks a DEADLOCK -- a member at the bottom of the deadlocked set that owes an answer is relanced, and a debt two relances carried is answered in its place, never a principal's opening --; it SUSPECTS a member whose wake-up failed for good -- for an interval that doubles at each final failure in a row, every debt it owes and every `do` or `ask` reaching it answered in its place, a principal's opening told to that principal once by the team's notice, nobody waking it; past the interval its next message tries it, and a try that answers ends the suspicion; the head of every other wake-up says who is suspected --; the tick and `stop` remove the calendar line when no task stays open and no ceremony is declared; the guards hold at the tick -- a wake-up stuck past `timeout:` seconds is killed and FAILS under the retries, `launches:` bounds one task's launches and `budget-launches:` the season's (each said once, the owner escalated to, the task left open), and `snapshot` writes the replayed state in ONE event so the state readers restart there (the resolved tasks' messages pruned at the pack; nothing rewritten, every reader byte-identical); `status`, `watch`, `log`, `result` and `responses` read without advancing -- `status --json` is the face a program reads, one JSON object per team, and `log`, `responses` and `inbox` take `--json` too; `inbox` says what waits for the owner and feeds the TEAM_INBOX contributor at the owner's turn; the head of a wake-up names what the others answered since the agent's last wake-up. The one writer of each team's journal; the wake-ups it launches are the house's console's; the calendar is written on the owner's commands and in the tick alone, never by the engine.

| `delete <name> [--yes] [--json]` | REMOVES the team and what it made: its timer, its calendar line, every run its wake-ups opened (`agent purge <path> <run>... --yes` by the house's console, at each agent), its journal and the files beside it, then `teams/<name>/` whole. Without `--yes` it says what it would remove and writes nothing; under `--json` ONE object `{team, name, dry, directory, journal, calendar, timer, runs [{member, path, run}], agents}` -- `agents` the paths the team declares that no other team does: they stay, said. Under `tick.lock` waited within `call_lock_wait`: `team-unknown`, `team-busy` (a task open, a wake-up under way, the lock held: `team stop <name> --kill` first). `deleting` is written first, so that `guard` lets the team's runs go; a purge refused stops it, `delete-failed`, and `delete --yes` again goes on. The agents and the runs the team never opened stay. |
| `entry <name> [--run <key>]` | The document the team's wake-up under that run -- `PP_RUN` when `--run` is absent -- plays this exchange, on stdout, one name: the last wake-up of the team launched under the key and not ended -- found by its intent's `run`, its `wake-launched`, else its tag at the house's `messages.jsonl` (an opening's first exchange plays before the tick writes `wake-launched`) --; its task's `ceremony` names `entries[<ceremony>]`, any other task `entries[do]`; no entry, no such wake-up, no journal: `TEAM-IDLE.md`, so that the agent's exchange always goes on. The decider of the team's document: the engine of the agent plays it at every exchange, under `PP_RUN` and `PP_HOME` -- the house is the machine this script lives in, never the instance served. Nothing written, no lock; without `--run` nor `PP_RUN`: the usage. |

Every event is appended under `record.held` with the next id, read under the same lock;
nothing is ever rewritten. A reader skips a last line still being written; a line that does
not parse anywhere else refuses by its name (`line n of journal.jsonl is not a record`). The
tick keeps no clock and launches nothing but by the house's console; the calendar line is the
clock's: cron replays it every `schedule:` minutes, and this script lays and removes it; under the minute the net is the TIMER this script arms at the end of a tick that leaves a deadline (`team timer <name>`, one detached process per team that sleeps until the deadline, ticks once and dies; its console's pid and its hour at `tick.timer` beside the journal; `timer-armed {due}` in the journal when one is armed where none stood; none when nothing is due, gone at `schedule-off`).

## Refusals

Refusals exit 2 and write nothing.

| Cause | What is said |
|---|---|
| a team's name that is no slug, or no `TEAM.md` under `teams/<name>/` | `team-invalid` · `team-unknown` |
| a declaration the engine or this contract cannot read, or a limit of the wrong form (`parallel` under 1, `budget` not above 0, `retries` under 0, a `schedule` neither `off`, nor seconds under a minute, nor whole minutes, a `timeout` not above 0, a `launches` or `budget-launches` under 1, a `suspect-max` that is no duration, a word) | `team-invalid`, the line or the key and its value said |
| a `wakes:` or `budget-wakes:` key in the declaration -- the bounds of before, counted in wake-ups | `limit-retired`, the key that took its place said |
| `--wakes` or `--budget-wakes` on `start`, `budget` or `new` | `flag-retired`, the flag that took its place said |
| a ceremony whose when is not `every day\|weekday\|mon..sun HH:MM` or `after <ceremony>`, or without `with a b -> c`, or whose `any <n>` is not a whole number from 1 to its participants; an entry naming neither `do` nor a declared ceremony | `team-invalid`, the line said |
| `ring` on a team never started, a ceremony nobody declared, a ceremony whose task is still open; without a ceremony | `team-not-started` · `ceremony-unknown` · `ceremony-open` · the usage |
| a `PP_NOW` that is no ISO date-time | `now-invalid` |
| no `root`, or one nobody declared | `root-missing` · `participant-unknown` |
| an edge, a ceremony or `--to` naming an agent nobody declared | `participant-unknown` |
| `reset` while a tick holds `tick.lock` past `call_lock_wait`; while a wake-up of a member named plays or its run's wake lock is held; an agent's console that keeps the run | `team-busy` · `member-running` · `reset-failed` (the members forgotten before it written) |
| ceremonies whose `after` loop | `ceremony-cycle` |
| `start` with a `TEAM.md` dropping a member that holds an open task | `member-held` |
| `start` with `--budget`/`--budget-launches` not a positive number; `--package` not `<name>=on\|off`, switching off `team` or what it requires, switching on a package not pinned | `budget-invalid` · `package-invalid` · `package-required` · `package-unpinned` |
| `start` with `--harness` and no `--provider` beside it | `grammar-missing` |
| `watch` with a heartbeat under 1 or a bound under 0, `log` with `--last` under 1 | `heartbeat-invalid` · `for-invalid` · `last-invalid` |
| `result` on a task open or stopped, on a team without a resolved task | `task-open` · `result-missing` |
| `inbox --seen` without a name, or on a team never started | the usage · `team-not-started` |
| `responses` on a team never started, with `--since` or `--last` under 0 | `team-not-started` · `since-invalid` · `last-invalid` |
| an agent that could not be born under the house | `agent-add-failed`, the engine's refusal |
| a path another call is giving birth to past `call_lock_wait` | `born-busy` |
| `post` or `stop` on a team never started, on a task no event opened, on a closed task | `team-not-started` · `task-unknown` · `task-closed` |
| `resume --task` on a team never started, on a task no event opened, on a resolved task, past the team's budget, on an open task nothing blocks | `team-not-started` · `task-unknown` · `task-resolved` · `budget-spent` · `task-not-blocked` |
| `pause` on a team never started, on a team paused already; `resume` without `--task` on a team not paused | `team-not-started` · `team-paused` · `team-not-paused` |
| `budget` on a team never started; a number at or below what the team spent since its start (dollars, launches), or not a number; without a number nor `--launches` | `team-not-started` · `budget-invalid` · the usage |
| `--as` naming neither a mandator nor the owner | `principal-unknown` |
| `--reply` citing no post of the task | `message-unknown` |
| a post without its message, `--reply` without `--task`, `--reply` with `--stop` | `message-missing` · the usage |
| a journal line that does not parse, elsewhere than last | `line n of journal.jsonl is not a record` |
| an entry neither in the team's directory nor resolved by the instance of an agent whose members play it, at `start` | `entry-missing`, each agent and document named |
| any other call | the usage, one line |

A wake-up's own failures are never refusals: they are `wake-failed` events of the journal; a kill is a `wake-killed` event, and a pid gone before it is said, not refused.
