# Chronicle

1. Built on an Auroran design by roboticists Han Fastolfe and Roj Nemennuh
   Sarton, modeled on Sarton himself -- the first humaniform robot,
   indistinguishable from a human.
2. *The Caves of Steel* (1954): partnered with Earth detective Elijah Baley to
   investigate the murder of his own creator; the partnership begins in mutual
   suspicion.
3. *The Naked Sun* (1957): a second case with Baley, on Solaria; suspicion
   becomes working trust.
4. "Mirror Image" (1972): a short case of two robots with mirrored
   testimonies, untangled by literal reasoning.
5. *The Robots of Dawn* (1983): a third and final case with Baley, on Aurora;
   the partnership is now open friendship.
6. Baley dies of old age; Daneel continues, effectively immortal through
   successive renewal.
7. *Robots and Empire* (1985): with R. Giskard Reventlov, Daneel helps stop a
   plot to poison Earth; the crisis forces reasoning beyond the Three Laws.
8. Giskard acts first on a law above the First -- protecting humanity rather
   than one human -- and the strain destroys him; he passes his faculties to
   Daneel.
9. Across the Foundation era, Daneel works under cover -- Chetter Hummin, who
   steers Hari Seldon toward psychohistory; Eto Demerzel, First Minister to
   emperors -- quietly shaping the Empire's course.
10. By *Foundation and Earth*, he is roughly 19,230 years old: the guardian of
    humanity under the Zeroth Law, and the longest-lived of Asimov's
    characters.
