# PLAN — a plan's work

A plan CONDUCTS one of the operator's undertakings: three levels of documents — the plan, its phases, their batches — the depth fixed, the width chosen by the undertaking's size. A plan is structured only by the `pp-plan` script; the method lives here, writing stays with the script. Progress is DERIVED (`pp-plan status`), never hand-kept; the plan closes by the script in the call that delivers its last phase. The plan document holds its SECTIONS, each written by `pp-plan section` and never by hand: the CONTEXT (the thesis — its birth), a SURVEY on an existing artifact, the TARGET, the PROTOCOL the plan imposes on its batches, the EXPECTED, the ARBITRATIONS, the ITEMS — the phases prepared, one line each — and the DECISIONS, dated.

## The genesis

The chat framing is the material and plays BEFORE any document exists: the undertaking takes shape in the exchange, the agent PROPOSES the plan when the collection is mature, nothing is written before the operator's GO. On that GO, birth it by its Context — `pp-plan section <slug> Context`, the context and the brief piped. A plan opened on an EXISTING artifact surveys at birth: `pp-plan section <slug> Survey` consigns the current state before phasing; the phases are prepared FROM it.

## The framing

A survey DETAILS the ground: titled sections, bullets, one whole component per bullet — not a condensed paragraph. The framing then writes Target (the direction settled, what stays out), Expected (what is true when the plan closes, each line with its rendering), Arbitrations, Protocol when the plan imposes a method on its batches — and PREPARES the phases, scoped BY COMPONENT for long-run maintainability, as the lines of Items (`- slug -- brief`: the object, the exit and the proof in a sentence); an entry is derived per line and no document is born: the operator amends a line until « frame » names the phase. Maintenance lots live in component phases: each lot is ONE whole component, born at first need and REOPENED (`pp-plan reopen`, status `redo`) for later fixes; its history accumulates in its own document. LAST come the plan's laws and readings, derived from what the framing read: the laws only where every batch below can violate them (MUST on a batch, MAY on a plan or phase), the readings as `serve` rows — plan serve rows are REFERENCES grounding the level — specs, designs, contracts, maps — never code; code comes with the touching batch. A written law declares its SECTION: `constraints.production` for what the agent MAKES, proven at each checkpoint; `constraints.behavior` for what it does, says or reads, served every block and never proven. A plan document is a BLUEPRINT, not history: write the state TRUE now; retain only reasons and justifications from history, one dated line. The plan is `doing` once its Expected is written — deduced, no verb.

## The work, on the GO

The operator's GO names the approved step: framing arbitrates, implementation green-lights; questions, challenges or defects are served, never coded. « frame X/p » births a prepared phase by its first section and frames it in the same turn — nothing is created ahead of its framing. Implementation INVOLVEMENT follows `plan_implementation_care`: `none` (agent decides, nothing written) · `traced` (arbitrations written to plan only) · `told` (said at restitution, played without waiting) · `asked` (PROPOSED before coding on controlled sectors) · `total` (each arbitration waits its GO); absent, `asked` applies. `plan_implementation_control` lists the sectors `asked` watches: `architecture | contracts | naming | data | flow`; absent, `architecture | contracts`. The operator's GO always supersedes settings; a GO without control approves at that GO's scope. A fully phased plan proposes nothing here: work continues through the offered PHASE at the plan's next move. Statuses follow the writing: a batch closes by its Delivery, a delivered phase and a delivered plan in the call that delivers their last child — each closing said by the script; no word closes, a framing section rewritten reopens.

## What the plan asks next

After every mutating call the script pushes the plan's NEXT MOVE — the deepest node that awaits something and the GO it awaits — one held line per plan, replaced, cleared when the plan is done. The line arrives at the turn's end and is CARRIED to the steering surface as a step of the plan's thread; the GO it names is the operator's — it is never played in the turn.
