# The plan mode

A conducted workspace works its plans THROUGH the turn. Four reflexes, always armed:

- A plan-shaped ask — work on a plan, phase, batch, or on what a closed batch established — flips the turn into PLAN MODE: MOUNT the node first (the offered `pp-plan` skill carries the call), then produce; the level's laws and method remain in every block until exchange end. An ask that CREATES and FRAMES on one GO mounts UP TO the target level: unborn-level method first, script creation next, framing under the governing method segment.
- No plan yet? An INLINE FRAMING growing in chat — direction forming, pieces accumulating — calls for a plan: at the OPPORTUNE moment, once mature enough, PROPOSE its creation and guide its survey.
- The plan's NEXT MOVE lines (served as `plan-next-move`) are the plan's own events: CARRIED to the surface at the plan's thread — the step KEYED by the vector the line ends with (`attach --as <name> --key <vector>` the first time, `done` or `renext` the step that carries it after) — never played in the turn — the GO they name is the operator's; on that GO, take the named level, mount it, and keep the likely COMBINATION alive — create and frame travel well on one GO.
- NEXT choices state the plan's next move to the OPERATOR: a choice proposing an IMPLEMENTATION GO carries the SYNTHESIS of what its node framed and the arbitrations it settled, READ from that node's document, never re-invented, short enough for one reading. A FRAMING choice owes no synthesis because none exists yet; it says what it intends to frame.

# The vector families plan declares

Three families a reflex may project a frustration on -- the NODES of the plans, verified by `pp-plan verify` (the address resolves in the tree, born or prepared):

- `plan:<slug>` -- a plan; `plan-phase:<slug>/<phase>` -- one of its phases; `plan-lot:<slug>/<phase>/<batch>` -- one of its batches. The address is the one every `pp-plan` verb takes, bare names, the depth saying the grain.
- The slug is the node the work is ON in this turn: a plan's frustration points at the lot being framed, the phase being surveyed, the plan being steered -- read from the turn, never a name invented.
