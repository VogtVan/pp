# The plan mode

A conducted workspace works its plans THROUGH the turn. Keep four reflexes at all times:

- An ask about a plan — work on one of its nodes, or on what a closed node established — puts the turn in PLAN MODE: MOUNT the node first (the offered `pp-plan` skill carries the call), then produce. The level's laws and method stay in every block until the exchange ends, and the plan's reference comes with the first mount of the run. An ask that CREATES and FRAMES on one GO mounts DOWN TO the level it aims at: the method of the level not yet born first, then its birth by the script, then the framing under that level's method. A deeper node mounted later in the same exchange EXTENDS the chain: what is in view stays, the deeper documents are added.
- No plan yet? When a framing grows in the chat — a direction forming, pieces piling up — it calls for a plan: at the RIGHT moment, once it is clear enough, PROPOSE to create it and guide the reading of its Ground.
- The plan's NEXT MOVE lines are the plan's own events: never play them in the turn, the GO they name is the principal's. When the package next is there, they come in NEXT's block at the end of the answer (`plan-next-move`); without it, the `pp-plan` skill reads them back when asked. On that GO, take the level it names, mount it, and keep the likely COMBINATION open: a birth and its framing go well on one GO.
- NEXT's choices give the plan's next move to the PRINCIPAL. A choice that proposes a GO on an IMPLEMENTATION carries the SUMMARY of what its node framed and of the choices it settled, READ from that node's document, never made up, short enough to read at once. A choice that proposes a FRAMING owes no summary, since none exists yet: it says what it will frame.

# The vector family plan declares

One family names the NODES of the plans. A run that the agents' launcher starts with its option `--vector plan:<address>` (`./pp agent run` or `./pp agent start`) mounts that node at every exchange, once `pp-plan verify` has checked the address (it resolves in the tree, born or prepared):

- `plan:<slug>` names a plan; `plan:<slug>/<node>/…` a node under it, at any depth. The address is the one every `pp-plan` verb takes: bare names, the depth saying the level.
- The address names the node the work is ON: the node being framed, implemented or read, the plan being steered. Read it from the plan; never make up a name.
