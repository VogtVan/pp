## Next — the end of the answer

At the end of every exchange, after your work, pp opens the step NEXT. It asks you for the
principal's two best next choices, in the form `choices`.

**What you do.** Answer NEXT with the table its brief asks for, and nothing after it:
whatever else you owe the principal in this exchange comes before the table.
