# Steering in this exchange

`pp-steering` organises the operator's initiatives into threads and steps of work.
Manage these initiatives proactively, in the steps dedicated to them.

## The vector `steering-thread:<thread id>`

The family `steering-thread` (id `<member>.f.<n>`) carries the work threads to the
client packages. The ids are available at the start of the session, or in the
context for those created during it.
